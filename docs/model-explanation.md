Our inventory replenishment model operates over a 21-day simulation period and differentiates between two types of SKU-store pairs based on demand behaviour.
For stable SKU-store pairs with consistent demand, the model calculates safety stock using statistical variability measures and sets a fixed reorder point based on average daily demand during lead time plus safety stock. Order quantities are fixed, covering forecasted demand for the next 7 days. Orders are triggered when inventory falls below this reorder point.
For SKU-store pairs exhibiting unusual or seasonal demand spikes, the model employs dynamic forecasting using the Holt-Winters exponential smoothing method to capture trends and seasonality. The reorder point is dynamically calculated as safety stock plus forecasted demand over the supplier lead time, updated daily. Economic order quantity (EOQ) varies with product shelf life—covering 7 days of demand for perishables with shelf life under 60 days, and 14 to 15 days for longer shelf life items. This ensures responsiveness to demand fluctuations and minimizes waste.
This differentiated approach optimizes inventory availability and turnover by matching replenishment policies to SKU demand characteristics, reducing lost sales, and improving operational efficiency.
